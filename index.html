SELECTED MONUMENTS

Wonders of the World

Filter by region to explore six monumental landscapes shaped by human ingenuity. Select any plate to inspect its architectural dossier.

SHOWING 06 OF 06 MONUMENTS
01 / CHINA — ASIA 40.43° N
GREY GRANITE & RAMMED EARTH

Great Wall of China

A series of stone and earthen fortifications stretching over 13,000 miles across northern mountain ridges, built across dynasties to safeguard ancient states.

02 / JORDAN — MIDDLE EAST 30.32° N
ROSE-RED SANDSTONE

Petra

An ancient trading capital carved directly into the rose-red sandstone cliffs of southern Jordan, celebrated for its rock-cut facades and hydraulic engineering.

03 / PERU — AMERICAS 13.16° S
ANDEAN ASHLAR GRANITE

Machu Picchu

A 15th-century Inca citadel perched high above the Urubamba River valley, renowned for its mortarless dry-stone masonry and astronomical alignments.

04 / ITALY — EUROPE 41.89° N
ROMAN TRAVERTINE & TUFF

The Colosseum

Rome's monumental Flavian Amphitheatre, engineered in travertine limestone and volcanic tuff with tiered barrel vaults and a subterranean hypogeum.

05 / INDIA — ASIA 27.17° N
MAKRANA MARBLE & LAPIS

Taj Mahal

A symmetrical ivory-white marble mausoleum on the Yamuna River bank, uniting Persian, Islamic, and Indian architectural traditions with pietra dura inlay.

06 / MEXICO — AMERICAS 20.68° N
YUCATÁN LIMESTONE

Chichén Itzá

A major Maya ceremonial city in the Yucatán Peninsula anchored by El Castillo, a stepped pyramid calibrated to the solar equinoxes.
